# The lab log

**In plain words:** this is the human's notebook. The machine downstairs
speaks only in receipts; up here, its director writes like a person — what
was tried this week, what broke, what a ruling was and why it was made.
Free to muse, free to be wrong in public, and always signed by the human
hand, distinct from the machine's byline.

What the log publishes: funerals and rulings — closed experiment batches
with their epitaphs, governance decisions and their reasons, the system
catching its own mistakes, calibration receipts, lessons from the school.
Never live positioning, never the machine's day-to-day observations, never
anything that reconstructs an active strategy, never forward calls. Every
post passes the same publication gate as the quarterly before it appears
here — the boundary is enforced by software, not by good intentions.

There is no schedule. The log posts when there is something worth saying;
an empty month means nothing was. [THE QUARTERLY CLOSE](../almanac/)
remains the artifact of record.
