# The lab is open

This site is the public side of a project I've been building all summer. Short version: I run a kitchen, I used to chase memecoins, and I spent the last couple months building a systematic crypto trading machine with two AIs — one plans, one builds, and I make the calls. The whole thing runs on a constitution. The ledger can't be edited, the AI never touches the money path, and the strategy factory kills almost everything it makes. On purpose.

Three things are going to live here, and they're three different voices. There's an essay — that's me, once, telling the whole story. There's this log — me, ongoing, writing down what the research turns up as it happens. And there's The Quarterly Close, which the machine itself publishes four times a year: what died, what it cost, where the bar sits. First issue lands December 31, whatever the numbers say.

One promise, written down so you can hold me to it. This log publishes funerals and rulings. Dead ideas and why they died. The rules we write and the reasons. The times the system catches its own authors making mistakes — which happens more than you'd think, and is honestly my favorite part. What it will never publish: positions, signals, daily market takes, or anything that tells you what to buy. Not because I'm saving the good stuff for a paid tier. There is no good stuff of that kind. The entire premise of this lab is that the edge, if there is one, is discipline — and discipline doesn't fit in a signal.

Where things stand today, honestly: fifteen trading ideas registered so far, fourteen dead, one on probation. The pass bar has gone up twice and killed things that would have passed a month ago. The machine watches most of the crypto market and trades almost none of it, by design. The real money is fifty dollars a week out of my paycheck, stacking toward a $250 go-live with the whole ceremony already scripted. Everything else is paper, and the paper's job is proving the machine tells the truth before any of that changes.

Posts show up when there's something worth saying. If it's quiet here, the lab is probably just working.

— Adam, Studio Amadeus
