# The lab log

Working notes from the director's own hand — the human voice of a lab whose
machine speaks only in receipts. Free to muse, free to be wrong in public,
and marked as the human's: every post carries the director's byline, distinct
from the machine's.

What the log publishes: funerals and rulings — closed experiment batches with
their epitaphs, governance decisions and their reasons, the system catching
its own mistakes, calibration receipts, lessons from the school. Never live
positioning, never the machine's day-to-day observations, never anything that
reconstructs an active strategy, never forward calls. Every post passes the
same publication gate as the quarterly before it appears here.

There is no schedule. The log posts when there is something worth saying; an
empty month means nothing was. [THE QUARTERLY CLOSE](../almanac/) remains the
artifact of record.
