# The school mirror

**In plain words:** the machine keeps a small library of lessons it teaches its own operator — one rides each daily digest, and one prints in the margin of every Quarterly Close. This wing mirrors the library's publishable subset (21 of 22 lessons; the remainder speak console vocabulary the public gate refuses, and are withheld rather than rewritten).

**The school stays free — law, not policy (TC-47a):** it was built under the director's own charter, and paywalling it would invert the charter.

**`backups-are-receipts`**

> The nightly copy is age-encrypted to a public key, so the automation never holds a secret. The receipts must outlive the disk — a ledger that can die with one machine was never a ledger.

**`benchmark-is-the-boss`**

> Law 5: every number renders beside hold-SOL, hold-USDC, and 50/50. If the machine can't beat sitting still, the dashboard says so — that honesty is the product, not a bug report.

**`breaker-not-bravery`**

> The daily-loss breaker flattens and freezes because the worst trades are made ten minutes after a drawdown. Re-arming requires a human on purpose — the delay IS the feature.

**`cascade-lowest-first`**

> Contributions route to the lowest tier below its fill target — DOCK before LINE before FIRE. Drawdown re-opens a tier's gap, so healing needs no special rule: the routing law IS the healing law.

**`deposits-are-events`**

> A deposit is an event with a basis stamp, not just a bigger number. From dollar one the ledger can reconstruct where every cent came from and what it cost — receipts before returns.

**`drawdown-is-the-price`**

> Max drawdown is the toll a strategy charges you to hold it. Two books with equal returns are not equal: the one that dug the shallower hole let you keep more nerve — and nerve is capital.

**`elections-not-polls`**

> Seated parameters re-run on their declared cadence, not when a trailing chart looks tempting. Chasing every interim leader is how a rule quietly becomes a mood (§16).

**`exits-never-blocked`**

> Entry rails protect capital before it's exposed; nothing may trap it once it is. That's why the slippage cap binds entries only — staying exposed is categorically worse than paying impact to leave.

**`fees-eat-small-accounts`**

> Every fill pays three tolls — router fee, spread, slippage. At this bankroll the tolls are why the rails cap trade frequency: an edge smaller than its costs is a loss with better marketing.

**`flat-is-a-position`**

> A flat day is not a missed day. Flat IS a position — the strategy judged today's odds unpaid and declined the fee. Most of a trend-follower's edge is the chop it refuses to trade.

**`graveyard-is-working`**

> Most FORGE hypotheses die. That's the machine working: search hard enough and beautiful backtests appear by chance, so the pass bar rises with every trial the registry has ever counted.

**`paper-is-honest-practice`**

> Paper fills come from real Jupiter quotes at real size, so the practice prices reality — but paper can't feel a failed transaction or a frozen route. Rung A's small live stake exists to teach what paper can't.

**`rank-your-costs`**

> Rotation decides weekly on purpose: each extra rebalance is a guaranteed cost paid to chase an unguaranteed rank. Cadence is a fee-control rail wearing a strategy costume.

**`regime-is-context`**

> Trend, chop, high-vol: the regime tag says which weather the day was, from thresholds hashed into every verdict. An edge that only exists in one regime isn't wrong — it just has to say so out loud.

**`rulings-leave-provenance`**

> A console ruling writes a signed event with via: console and announces itself on CRITICAL. The bench tells on itself by design — authority you can audit beats authority you remember granting.

**`sharpe-plainly`**

> Sharpe is return per unit of wobble. A 0.9 isn't 'good' by decree — it's only meaningful beside the benchmark's own wobble over the same window, which is why margins ship with confidence intervals.

**`slippage-vs-impact`**

> Impact is what your size does to the price; slippage is what the market does between quote and fill. The rails cap them on entries only — a mandated exit always takes the fill and records the bruise.

**`small-size-is-tuition`**

> At this bankroll a spectacular year is dinner money. The asset being built is the pipeline and the discipline — capital scales into a proven machine, never hope into an unproven one.

**`tax-wedge`**

> An active book must beat the benchmark by the tax wedge just to tie: short-term gains are taxed as income while the passive ruler compounds toward long-term rates. The referee prices that; hope doesn't.

**`two-receipts`**

> Every money movement has two receipts: the chain's signature and the ledger's row. If they ever disagree the system halts and screams — Law 4 is a tripwire, not a promise.

**`verdicts-not-vibes`**

> Reviews fire on the clock — 182 days or ~60 trades — never on a hot week. CLEAN means beat the benchmark after the tax wedge; two CLEANs open the fed-capital door. The ladder has no feelings.
