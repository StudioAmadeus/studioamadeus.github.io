# The museum

**In plain words:** the machine's history and its laws, on public exhibit — everything that has already happened and everything already ruled. What never hangs here: the living tape. No positions, no balances, no current strategy internals, no daily feed — those are barred by the same publication law that governs everything else on this site.

Exhibits are compiled from the ledger at boundaries and closes — a museum, not a ticker.
