# THE QUARTERLY CLOSE — the EXPO almanac

The machine's quarterly, written from its own ledger: what died in the
strategy forge and why, the rising pass bar, measured all-in costs at retail
size, verdicts with config-hash receipts, the period's regime tag, and one
lesson in the margin. The graveyard, not the returns.

The rules it is printed under:

- **A ledger close, not an editorial deadline.** Issues print at calendar
  quarter closes. An empty quarter prints as an empty quarter — flat is a
  position for the publication too.
- **Immutable once published.** Corrections print as errata in the next
  issue, never edits.
- **Fixed template, every issue.** Period close → measured-cost table →
  verdicts with receipts → regime tag → lesson. No silent metric
  redefinitions; schema changes carry errata lines.
- **Educational, never advisory.** The issue reports what died, what it
  cost, and what was measured. Hashes give proof without disclosure.
- **Free.** The cumulative registry table is free forever.
