# Studio Amadeus

A small lab building a deterministic trading machine with airtight receipts —
and publishing what most operations hide: the hypotheses that died, the
measured costs at retail size, and the pass bar that rises with every attempt.

Nothing here recommends anything. Educational, never advisory: no signals, no
forward calls, no course at the end of it.

**[The essay](#the-essay)** — the human's story, told once. *Not yet

**[The lab log](log/)** — the director's working notes, ongoing. A notebook,

**[THE QUARTERLY CLOSE](almanac/)** — the machine's quarterly, compiled from
