# Studio Amadeus

**In plain words:** this is a one-person lab that built a trading machine —
and the machine keeps receipts for everything it does. It watches the crypto
market around the clock, tests precise trading ideas against years of real
price history, and follows written rules a human cannot override on a whim.
So far it trades only with practice money, and it may never earn the right
to do more. That is not a failure; that is the experiment.

Most trading publications show you their wins. We publish the opposite: the
ideas that failed and why, what trading actually costs at small size, and
the discipline that keeps human impulses out of the loop.

Nothing here recommends anything. Educational, never advisory: no signals,
no forward calls, no course at the end of it.

**[The lab log](log/)** — the director's working notes, ongoing. A notebook,
not a feed: it posts when there is something worth saying, and it is allowed
to think out loud and be wrong.

**[THE QUARTERLY CLOSE](almanac/)** — every three months, the machine prints
a report card on itself, straight from its own records: which experiments
died, what everything cost, what the market's weather was. Receipts only;
never edited after printing. First close: **2026-12-31**.
